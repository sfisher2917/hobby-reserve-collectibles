Privacy policy

Effective November 12, 2026.

Hobby Reserve Inventory Sync

This application is used to retrieve and display product listings from the Hobby Reserve Collectibles owner's eBay seller account. Its purpose is to display current inventory, including listing titles, images, descriptions, prices and availability, with purchases completed on eBay.

The current integration retrieves listing information directly from eBay on request. It does not persistently store new eBay listing responses in a database, saved catalog, or application logs. Responses are processed temporarily in server memory and displayed in the visitor's current browser page. API responses instruct browsers and intermediaries not to cache them.

The integration does not request buyer profiles, bidder identities, orders, transactions or customer account details. It does not collect or retain buyer information or other eBay users' personal information. Only the owner's public seller identifier and the product fields needed for inventory display are requested. Seller authorization credentials are protected separately on the server and are not sent to website visitors.

Earlier inventory snapshots

Before this request-time integration, listing snapshots were saved. The current published catalog copies have been emptied and scheduled snapshot exports are disabled. Historical repository commits, prior deployment archives and earlier private review copies may still contain previous public listing data. Historical cleanup is not yet complete.

Website and CardScout

The storefront does not require a customer account or process payments. CardScout can save watchlists and preferences in the visitor's own browser; these are separate from eBay seller inventory. Hosting providers may process normal technical access logs to operate and secure the website. The application does not write eBay response bodies or buyer information into its logs.

External services

Product photos can load from eBay. Following an eBay or Instagram link takes you to that service, whose own policies apply. eBay handles checkout, shipping addresses, payment information and purchase records.
